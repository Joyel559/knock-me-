## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Knock Me is a travel alarm for people who fall asleep on the road. Set your destination once.
Knock Me watches your GPS in the background and rings loudly when you get close to your stop, so
you never miss it. If your cab leaves the planned route, it rings again, so you can act before a
wrong turn becomes a problem.

It was built for real trips: long bus and train rides where you doze off and don't know if
you've reached your stop, cab rides late at night where you don't want to stare at a map the
whole way, and anyone who just wants to rest without worrying they'll miss their destination.

TWO ALARMS, ONE APP
• Arrival alarm — rings loudly as you approach your destination, even with the screen locked
  and the phone on silent.
• Wrong-route alarm — rings if your cab drifts more than 500 m off the route you planned, so a
  detour doesn't go unnoticed.

NO-API-KEY SEARCH AND PASTE
Search for a destination by name, pin it on the map, or paste a Google Maps link, shared text,
plain coordinates, or a Plus Code — Knock Me reads the destination straight out of it, no
account or API key required anywhere.

SAVED PLACES AND HISTORY
Save your common destinations (Home, Office, anywhere), reuse your recent trips with one tap,
and keep a simple local history of where you've been — all stored on your phone.

BATTERY-FRIENDLY BY DESIGN
Knock Me checks your location less often when you're far from your destination and more often as
you get close, so it doesn't drain your battery on a long ride.

LEAN PERMISSIONS
A fresh install only asks for location while you use the app and notifications — nothing more.
An optional "Max reliability" mode exists for phones that aggressively kill background apps, and
it's entirely opt-in.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your location never leaves your device except for the exact
text or coordinates needed to answer a search or routing request you made yourself, sent to the
free, public OpenStreetMap-based services that power search and routing.
